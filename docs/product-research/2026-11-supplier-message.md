2-in-1 pannier backpack (bike bag that converts to a backpack). Before ordering a first
batch I'd like to know:

1. Unit price for 20, 50 and 100 pieces (EXW and DDP to the Netherlands).

4. Rack mounting system: does it fit standard Dutch bike racks (tube diameter 8–16 mm)?

5. Is there a padded laptop sleeve? What is the maximum laptop size?
6. Fabric composition (for the EU textile label) and whether it is PVC-free.
7. Can you provide manufacturer details and safety info for the EU GPSR?
8. Lead time and shipping time to the Netherlands for 20 pieces.
9. Sample price and shipping cost for 2 samples (in 2 different colours if available).
10. Minimum order for my own logo and custom packaging.

Thank you, I look forward to your reply.
