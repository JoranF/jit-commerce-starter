# CJ sourcing request: Lichtval wall lamp (water ripple)

Goal: get a real factory price and delivery time, so we know if the break-even works.
Today we only have an AliExpress estimate (~$63), which gives a break-even CPA of about €40 at €119.
A CJ price of **$30–40** would raise that to about €60–70.

## How to send it (about 10 minutes)

1. Make a free account at cjdropshipping.com (personal details; a company is not needed yet for a request).
2. In the dashboard go to **Sourcing → Post Sourcing Request** (the name can differ slightly).
3. Fill in the fields with the text below. Add 2–3 pictures: screenshots of the reference products
   (the lamp on a wall with the light pattern, plus a close-up of the reflector).
4. CJ usually answers in 1–3 working days. Put the answer in the table at the bottom of this file.
