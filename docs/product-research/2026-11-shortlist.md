### Competitor price ladder (NL/BE)

- Lidl Crivit bike backpack (DE, Aug 2026): €14,99, basic
- Basil Flex €46–62 · **Basil SoHo Nordlicht 2-in-1 €89,95** (closest competitor)
- ANWB 2-in-1 €54,99 (members) / €64,99 · Decathlon marketplace 2-in-1 €59,99–89,99 · Valkental €67,99
- Vaude Cycle 28 / Aqua Transformer €115–140 · Ortlieb Vario €155–200

**Position:** €79,95, i.e. design + real waterproofing (IPX rating on the page) at a price under Basil SoHo
and well under Vaude/Ortlieb. Name the store around Dutch rain/commuting, not around "gadget".

### Break-even at €79,95

| | |
|---|---|
| Ex VAT (÷1,21) | €66,08 |
| Product ⚠️ (Alibaba ~$20–24 at 1–100 pcs) | €21 |
| Inbound freight per unit ⚠️ | €4 |
| Shipping to customer (PostNL parcel) ⚠️ | €5 |
| Fees + returns allowance (~4%) | €3,20 |
| Customer pays €4,95 shipping (ex VAT) | +€4,09 |
| **Break-even CPA** | **≈ €37** (NL Meta e-com CPA ≈ €30–45) |
| Break-even ROAS | ≈ 2,2 |

Upsells: rain cover, reflective strap, laptop sleeve → raises order value without more ad cost.

### Supplier reality

No EU-warehouse dropship supplier found. Realistic routes:
1. **Test with a small stock order**: 10–30 pcs from Alibaba (e.g. Rhinowalk-type, MOQ 1–49 at ~$24) → €300–800, ships to NL in ~2–3 weeks. Logo/custom packaging only from 500 pcs.
2. Ship-from-China dropship for the first ad test (cheaper, but 7–15 days delivery hurts reviews).

## Break-even (top 3)

Formula from the skill: price ex VAT − landed cost − fees = max ad cost per sale (break-even CPA).
NL Meta e-commerce cost per purchase is roughly €30–45 ⚠️.

|---|---|---|---|
| Selling price incl. VAT | €64,95 | €69,95 | €59,95 |
| Ex VAT (÷1,21) | €53,68 | €57,81 | €49,55 |
| Product cost ⚠️ | €16 | €22 | €18 |
| Shipping to customer ⚠️ | €5 | €6 | €5 |
| Fees + returns allowance (~4%) | €2,50 | €2,60 | €2,30 |
| Shipping paid by customer (€4,95 → ex VAT) | +€4,09 | +€4,09 | +€4,09 |

All three are borderline-OK. Getting the supplier price down or adding a bundle/upsell is what
turns them profitable.

## Store brief — #1 wake-up light (draft)

- **Name ideas:** Ochtendgloren · Zachtwakker · Lumé
- **Customer:** 25–45, works office hours, hates the dark alarm in Nov–Feb, often a partner who
  wakes up at a different time.
- **The one problem:** waking up in the dark feels awful; a beeping alarm makes it worse.
- **Hero promise (NL):** "Word wakker met zonsopkomst — ook in januari."
- **Design direction:** hand to the `frontend-design` skill: warm dawn gradient used once (hero),
  calm bedroom photography, avoid the generic cream + terracotta look.
- **Meta ad angles:** 1) before/after morning (phone alarm vs. light) 2) couple: one wakes, the
  other sleeps on 3) "07:00 en nog pikkedonker" relatable hook.
- **Higgsfield shots:** product hero on bedside table; bedroom lifestyle at dawn; 15s video
  showing the light slowly rising; close-up of the material/design.
